# Cosmic Diary Collage

> **[Generate this style in PixPix →](https://www.pixpix.com/?source=github63)**

[中文](README.md)

An original `玩趣收藏` image-making Skill. It keeps the upper part of a photograph recognisable, then rebuilds the lower panel as paper planets, a star chart-like abstraction and a blank diary card. The goal is celestial imagination without astrology claims.

## Three text-free examples

![Lilac spin: photo and cosmic diary collage](examples/sample-01-lilac-spin.png)

![Disco window: photo and cosmic diary collage](examples/sample-02-disco-window.png)

![Silver waterfront: photo and cosmic diary collage](examples/sample-03-silver-waterfront.png)

## Best for

night colour, reflective material, movement and mood. Its default tone is dreamy and playful, with midnight, lilac, silver, peach and the tactile character of foil fleck, dark paper, soft pastel.

## Use

`Use $photo-to-cosmic-diary-collage to process this photo at editorial density, focusing on one detail that matters to me.`

## Install in Codex

1. Clone or download this repository: `git clone https://github.com/Jack-chen-cyber/awesome-memory-image-skills.git`.
2. Copy the complete `skills/photo-to-cosmic-diary-collage` directory into Codex's Skills directory; on Windows, the default is `%USERPROFILE%\.codex\skills\photo-to-cosmic-diary-collage`.
3. Restart or reload Codex, then invoke `$photo-to-cosmic-diary-collage` in a conversation.

Do not copy only `SKILL.md`; keep `agents/`, `LICENSE.md`, and the page files with the Skill.
