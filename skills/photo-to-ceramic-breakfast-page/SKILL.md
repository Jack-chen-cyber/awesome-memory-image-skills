# Ceramic Breakfast Page / 陶瓷早餐页

Create one original 4:5 vertical composition from a photograph. Keep the upper 52–60% recognisably photographic, with only gentle cleanup. Rebuild the lower panel as an authored visual interpretation; it must not be a generic template or a copied style.

## Creative direction

- **Source cues:** mug, plate, fruit, linen and morning light.
- **Lower panel:** a tabletop still life with individual ceramic and fruit studies.
- **Palette:** cream, tomato, cobalt.
- **Materials:** matte glaze, linen, thick paper.
- **Tone:** slow and nourishing.

Use no more than five visual ideas in the lower panel. Let one idea be the anchor, give the others room, and make the resulting image feel like a breakfast post with editorial calm.

## User controls

Ask for or infer a mood, a preferred colour, and one detail that matters to the user. Offer one of three density choices: `quiet` (3 objects), `editorial` (4–5 objects), or `collected` (6–7 small objects only when the source supports it).

## Guardrails

- Preserve real people, personal identity, and major scene geometry in the upper panel.
- Do not recreate logos, package designs, ticket text, book pages, signage, maps, artworks, artist signatures, or a named creator's visual style. Replace them with original blank or abstract forms.
- Do not invent readable typography. Keep any card, label, map, receipt, ticket, or letter shape blank unless the user explicitly supplies non-infringing exact copy.
- Remove or soften faces, precise addresses, routes, booking details, plates, and other private identifiers when appropriate.
- Avoid watermarks, fake handwriting, generic travel icons, excessive tape, and overcrowded sticker layouts.

Deliver one image and a short note naming the source cues that were transformed.
